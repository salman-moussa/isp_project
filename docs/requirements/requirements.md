## How to use this catalogue

Requirement IDs are permanent. A requirement may be clarified but not renumbered or reused. A
removed requirement remains with status `Withdrawn` and a rationale. `Must` is release-blocking
unless an approved exception is recorded in [assumptions and risks](assumptions-and-risks.md). The
implementation and verification locations below are plans until evidence links replace them in the
[traceability matrix](traceability.md).

Acceptance criteria use Given/When/Then semantics even when compressed into one sentence. The global
Definition of Done applies to every requirement: tenant authorization, audit where sensitive, EN/AR
and RTL where user-facing, loading/error/empty/permission states, accessibility, observability,
migration and rollback impact, and risk-proportionate automated tests.

## Product-level release acceptance

The final release gate is the conjunction of `PRD-QLT-002`, every `Must` requirement not formally
excepted, the phase gates in [the task plan](../task-plan.md), and the following mandatory journeys:

1. Platform lead → accepted quote → payment → idempotent provisioning → live tenant.
2. Platform monthly billing → grace → controlled restriction → eligible payment → restoration, with
   no subscriber-service effect.
3. Approved, visible, scoped, expiring support access and revocation.
4. Tenant subscriber onboarding → billing/payment → installation/network activation.
5. Recurring billing and correction chains under concurrent/retry faults.
6. Office and offline collector payments through reconciliation with printer and sync failures.
7. Safe MikroTik single and bulk workflows through uncertain outcomes and reconciliation.
8. Staging deployment, backup, isolated restore, smoke, and rollback exercises.

## Change control

Changes require a short proposal naming affected IDs, rationale, compatibility/data impact,
security/tenancy impact, migration and rollback, test changes, and approvers. Architecture changes
also require an ADR. Financial invariants, tenant isolation, support-access controls, and the
subscriber-service/platform-subscription boundary cannot be waived by a single implementer.
