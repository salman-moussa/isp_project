Status: Live register

through an ADR or requirements change.

## Risk scoring

Likelihood (`L`) and impact (`I`) are 1–5. Score = L×I: Critical 20–25, High 12–19, Medium 6–11, Low
1–5. Residual score is reassessed after controls are evidenced, not merely planned.

## Accepted-risk record template

An acceptance must name risk ID, current/residual evidence, precise exception and duration,
compensating control, accountable business owner, technical/security reviewers, expiry/review date,
and rollback/remediation task. Critical/high security risks cannot be accepted by the implementing
author alone.
